    When it feels easy

    • Feet up on a step. It shifts more of your weight onto your arms, so every push-up is heavier.
    • Slow your squats. Three seconds down keeps the muscle working longer.
    • One leg at a time. Half the legs, twice the load.

    Keep it on your Home Screen

    In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app and remembers your day.

    The routine comes from a Neurolab reel on training without a gym. One study found push-ups build the chest about as well as the bench press (Kikuchi & Nakazato, 2017). This is not medical advice. Ease in and stop if something hurts.
